Work the Pipeline

Keep the dialer on for most of the day. Your job is to get as many owners as possible to answer. A new lead gets a call inside 5 minutes, and everything else is dialed in the order below. When two things compete, the higher one wins.

The texts and reminders go out automatically. You dial. The first call to a new lead is a double dial: no answer, hang up, call right back. Owners pick up most before the bays fill (8 to 9), at lunch (11:30 to 1), and near close (4 to 6), their time.
